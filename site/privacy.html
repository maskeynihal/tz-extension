Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

TZ Converter collects no data. It makes no network requests, contains no analytics or tracking, and sends nothing off your device. Everything it does runs locally in your browser.

What the extension does

TZ Converter converts timezones. You select a time on a web page, or enter one in the popup, and it shows the equivalent time in the zones you choose. All parsing and conversion happens on your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. There is no server, no account, and no telemetry.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves your preferences using the browser's own storage:

  • Your home timezone and chosen target timezones
  • 12- or 24-hour clock preference
  • Theme (System / Light / Dark)
  • How to treat a date with no time
  • Any custom date formats you add, and which built-in formats you switched off
  • Whether the converter opens automatically when you select text

This data lives only in your browser. If you have browser sync enabled, your browser may sync it across your own devices through your browser account — we never see it and have no access to it. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

The website's online converter and world clock keep the same kind of preferences (timezones, 12/24-hour clock, theme, day/month order) in your browser's localStorage for this site, so they are there next time. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Permissions

  • storage — to remember the preferences listed above, locally.
  • contextMenus — adds the right-click item Convert “…” with TZ Converter for selected text.
  • activeTab and scripting — when you click that item in a tab where the extension is not running yet (for example a tab opened before you installed or updated it), the extension loads its converter into that one tab. This only happens after your click, only in that tab.
  • Access to web pages — the "select to convert" feature needs to run on the page you are reading so it can read the text you select and show a popover. It reads only your active selection to parse a time; it does not read, collect, or send page content. You can turn automatic select-to-convert off in Settings and use only the right-click item.

Third parties

There are no third parties. The extension bundles all its code at build time and loads nothing from the network at runtime.
