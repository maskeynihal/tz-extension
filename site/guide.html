How to use TZ Converter

Everything the extension and this website can do, and how to do it. The extension converts times where you read them; the website has an online converter and a world clock for planning across zones. Both run entirely in your browser.

Quick start

  1. Install it from the Chrome Web Store (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or Firefox Add-ons.
  2. Pin the TZ icon from your browser's extensions menu, then open it once and set your home timezone and the zones you want to see in Settings.
  3. Select any time on a page — 1730000000, 2026-10-28T04:24:00Z, 22.03.2024 14:05, “Wednesday, October 28, 2026, 04:24 UTC”. A card appears next to it with that time in your zones.

No install? The online converter runs the same engine in a tab.

Site access

To convert what you select, the extension needs permission to read the pages you visit. It reads only the text you select, and nothing leaves your device.

  • Chrome and Edge grant it when you install. If you narrowed it to “On click”, the popup shows a banner with an Allow on all sites button.
  • Firefox may install with access withheld, especially when the add-on is loaded by hand. The extension then opens its settings page with the same Allow on all sites button, and the toolbar icon shows a ! badge until access is granted.
  • Prefer to allow only some sites? Grant them from the browser's own menu and choose Don't show again on the banner; the badge goes away too.

Select to convert

Select text that contains a date or time. If it reads as one, a card opens beside your selection.

  • Hundreds of layouts: Unix seconds and milliseconds, ISO 8601, RFC dates, numeric dates in any order and separator, compact 19990322, week and ordinal dates, the Unix date output, and written dates in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Chinese and Japanese.
  • Zones in the text are honoured: Z, offsets like +05:45, GMT+5:30, [Europe/Paris], and capitalised abbreviations like EST, CET, IST. A time with no zone is read in your home zone.
  • Day or month first? For a date like 03/04/2024, the card lets you pick the reading and remembers your choice.
  • Several times at once: select a paragraph and the card shows a stepper, ‹ 2 of 3 ›. Step through them; the mark on the page follows.
  • Your selection stays: you can still copy everything you selected.
  • Quiet when it's not a time: selecting ordinary text shows nothing.

Right-click to convert

Select text, right-click, and choose Convert “…” with TZ Converter. This works even with automatic conversion switched off, and it says so when the selection holds no time it recognises.

Conversion cards

  • Your zones, side by side, each with its date, offset and difference from home.
  • Drag a card by its header to move it.
  • Compact or full: switch the card size; Settings decides how cards open (compact, full or last used).
  • Remove a zone from a card with its ×.
  • See which format matched the text, and switch it off in Settings if you don't want it.
  • Close with the × or Esc.

Pins and the dock

  • Pin to page keeps a card on the page while you work. New pins take a free spot near their text and never move the ones already pinned.
  • Once something is pinned, a small dock appears with Minimize all, Expand all, Unpin all (float them so they stay in view) and Close all, which you can undo.
  • Pin all to page puts floating cards back next to their text.

Keyboard

The shortcut is a browser extension shortcut, so it never blocks a page's own keys. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (Edge: edge://extensions/shortcuts) or, in Firefox, Add-ons and themes → ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts.

Popup converter

Click the TZ icon in the toolbar.

  • Enter a date and time and pick the zone it's in; read it in every target zone. Now fills in the current time.
  • Daylight saving is handled, including times that don't exist (spring forward) or happen twice (fall back) — the popup tells you which one it used.
  • Switch between 12- and 24-hour clocks.
  • The clock button opens the world clock with your zones already in it.
  • The gear opens Settings.

Epoch converter

  • Paste a Unix timestamp. Seconds or milliseconds are detected automatically, and you can override it.
  • See it in your zones and as ISO 8601 UTC.
  • Go the other way: turn a date back into an epoch.

Formats

Every built-in format is listed in Settings → Formats with the pattern it matches, and any of them can be switched off. Add your own with tokens, and check them in the live tester:

PatternMatches
DD/MM/YYYY HH:mm22/03/2024 14:05
MMM D, YYYY h:mm AMar 22, 2024 2:05 PM
DD.MM.YYYY [um] HH:mm22.03.2024 um 14:05
yyyy-MM-dd'T'HH:mm:ssXXX2024-03-22T14:05:00+05:45 (Java / Talend style)

Tokens include YYYY, MM, MMM, DD, Do, HH, hh with A, mm, ss, SSS, TZ, weekday names and [literal text]. Advanced users can write a regular expression with named groups instead. The full token list is in the README.

Timezones

Every zone picker is searchable and grouped:

  • Cities — every IANA zone, such as Kathmandu, New York, London.
  • Fixed offsets — UTC+05:45, UTC−08:00, no daylight saving.
  • Abbreviations — PDT, EDT, CET, IST, AEST and more.
  • US zones that follow daylight saving — PT, MT, CT, ET.

Online converter

Open the converter, paste any time — an epoch, an ISO string, a day-first date — and read it across your zones. Search any city, offset or abbreviation to add a zone. Your zones and clock format are saved in this browser.

World clock

Open the world clock for a board of live clocks, one card per zone.

  • Each card shows the time, date, Today / Tomorrow / Yesterday, offset, difference from home, day or night, and whether it's working hours there.
  • Add a timezone from the search box; remove one with its ×.
  • Reorder by dragging a card's handle, or with Alt + arrow keys.
  • Make home moves any zone to the first card; differences are measured from it.
  • 12h / 24h, System / Light / Dark, and full screen for a wall display.
  • From the extension: the popup's clock button opens this page with your extension zones, carried in the link's # part, which is never sent to any server.

Plan a time

Find a time that works across zones. Every way of planning moves the same instant, so all the clocks always agree.

The main slider

  • Drag Shift all clocks to move every clock together, from 12 hours back to 36 hours ahead. It moves your home zone's time — the label next to it names the zone.
  • Arrow keys move 15 minutes, Page Up/Page Down one hour.
  • The marks under it (−12h … +36h) jump straight there.

Pick any date and time

  • Pick date & time opens a calendar and a time field, read as your home zone's clock. Use it for any day, weeks or months away.
  • After a pick, the main slider keeps working: it shifts around the picked time, and its centre mark reads Pick.
  • If the time you pick is skipped or repeated by daylight saving, the planner tells you which moment it used.

A slider on every card

  • Each card has its own slider covering that zone's day, midnight to midnight, with working hours shaded.
  • Move it to set the time in that zone; every other card and the main slider follow.
  • Example: pick 8 PM at home, then drag the UTC card to 5 PM to see what that means everywhere else.

Now (or Esc inside the planner) returns every clock to the live time.

Privacy

Nothing you select, type or plan leaves your device. The extension makes no network requests and has no analytics; its settings live in your browser's extension storage. The website keeps its settings in this browser's local storage. Read the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

Selecting a time does nothing

Check site access first: a ! on the toolbar icon means it's withheld. Also check that Convert when I select text is on in Settings → General, and that the format isn't switched off in Settings → Formats. Browser pages (settings, the web stores) never allow extensions to read them.

A page I already had open doesn't convert

Reload it once. After you grant access, the extension also tries to reach tabs that were already open.

03/04 reads as the wrong date

Pick the other reading on the card, or set Numeric date order in Settings → Cards.

The keyboard shortcut doesn't work

Another extension may own the key. Set a new one in your browser's extension shortcuts (see Keyboard); the card's hint says so when none is assigned.

My own date format isn't recognised

Add it in Settings → Formats with tokens, and paste a sample into the tester to check it matches.
