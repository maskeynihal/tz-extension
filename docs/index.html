Everything you need to read a time right.

Every feature works where you already are — on the page, in a popup, or in a full-page world clock.

✶

Select to convert

Highlight any time on any page and a small card shows it in your zones, right next to the text. Your selection stays put, so Ctrl/⌘ C still copies exactly what you highlighted. Prefer no pop-ups? Turn it off and right-click a selection instead.

⧖

100+ date formats

Epochs (seconds or milliseconds), ISO 8601 with week and ordinal dates, European day-first dates like 22.03.2024, compact 19990322, Unix date output and more. When 03/04/2024 could be either, pick day-first or month-first once. Add your own formats with a live tester.

⌕

Search every zone

Find zones by city, region, offset or abbreviation: Kathmandu, UTC+05:45, PDT, EDT. Drag your zones into the order you want, or move them with Alt+arrow keys.

⌖

Pin and compare

Pin cards to compare several times at once, each with its own colored highlight. A pin dock minimizes, expands, unpins or closes them all in one click. Cards open compact, full, or at the size you used last.

◴

World clock

A full-page board of your zones with day and night, the gap from home, and working hours at a glance. Drag the time scrubber to plan a meeting across every zone. Open the world clock →

◳

Converter & settings

A popup converter with a date picker and an epoch tab, 12/24-hour and DST-correct. Settings are grouped into tabs — General, Cards, Timezones, Formats — on one scrolling page.

Install in a minute.

Get it from your browser's add-on store. Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers install it from the Chrome Web Store.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Add it

Open the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons page and click Add. Updates arrive automatically.

Pin it

Pin the TZ icon from your browser's extensions menu so the converter is one click away.

Use it

Select any time on a page, or click the toolbar icon for the full converter. That's it.

Or load it unpacked from the latest release: unzip tz-extension-chrome.zip and use chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked, or unzip tz-extension-firefox.zip and use about:debugging → Load Temporary Add-on → pick manifest.json.
